Everything your store needs

Everything below ships in OZ. Three extras are optional $5 add-ons.

Included

Java & Bedrock login

Players pick their edition, type their name and see their own skin before they buy. Bedrock names get your Geyser prefix automatically.

How: pick Login editions and set Bedrock username prefix (e.g. a dot) in the theme settings.
Add-on · $5

3D rank preview

Rank pages show the player's real skin in 3D with the rank's nametag, plus how they'll look in chat and the tab list. Players switch ranks with one click.

How: set Rank category and write your prefixes and colours in Rank styles.
Included

Quantity buttons

Keys, coins and crates get ×1 ×5 ×10 ×15 buttons. The button shows the total before the player adds it.

How: list the categories in Quantity categories and pick your amounts in Quantity options.
Included

Basket drawer

The basket slides in from the side, so players keep shopping. Items, quantities, total and checkout in one place.

How: on by default. Add a Free-gift goal to show a progress bar in the basket.
Add-on · $5

Ready-made pages

Staff, Vote, FAQ, Patrons, Rules and News pages with Minecraft skins and copy buttons. Create a Tebex page with that title and OZ styles it.

How: one line per entry, e.g. Notch | Owner | Notch.
Included

Setup page that does the typing

A checklist shows what's missing, and editors build the text for staff, votes, FAQ, slider and rank table. Copy, paste into settings, done.

How: turn Setup page on, create a Tebex page called Setup. Turn it off when you're finished.
Included

290 pixel icons

Crowns, keys, coins, crates, pets, swords and more for your categories and packages. Your own package images still work.

How: Ranks=crown-gold, Keys=key-diamond in Category icons.
Add-on · $5

Sales that sell

Homepage slider with images or YouTube, a sale bar with a live countdown, a one-time discount pop-up and a currency picker.

How: switch each on in settings: slides, sale, promo, currencies.
